Accountable by Design

Every pound is handled carefully

POI is a not-for-profit company limited by guarantee (Company Number 17391401). Our published Financial Controls Policy, approved by Director Vivian Okolie (FCA, ACMA, CGMA), sets out how money is managed:

  • Payments over £100 need two independent signatories
  • Clear spending limits, with anything over £500 agreed by a majority of the board
  • Strict conflict-of-interest rules and incident reporting
