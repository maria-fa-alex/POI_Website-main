Open Roles

Lend your voice

We are looking for enthusiastic volunteers passionate about youth empowerment, debate coaching, and community support.

Role 01 Open

Debate Judge

Evaluate parliamentary debates, offer constructive feedback, and guide aspiring debaters. Previous BP or competitive debate experience preferred.

Apply as Judge →
Role 02 Open

Workshop Mentor

Lead 30-minute skills workshops, facilitate practice exercises, and teach critical thinking, argumentation, and rebuttal techniques to secondary students.

Apply as Mentor →
Role 03 Open

Guest Speaker

Share your insights from law, philosophy, politics, academia, or industry. Inspire students on the power of effective communication in the real world.

Apply as Guest Speaker →
Role 04 Applications closed

Director

Directors provide strategic leadership and oversight of the organisation. They help set direction, make key decisions, and ensure the organisation is effective and sustainable, while ensuring full regulatory and legal compliance.

Applications are currently closed for the Director role. However, you are welcome to get in touch to register your interest for future recruitment cycles.
